November at NerdzFactory Company

NerdzFactory Company works with organisations, institutions and development partners on digital skills, entrepreneurship, youth innovation, digital safety and workforce development. This edition is a snapshot of November: digital skills graduates moving into internships, innovation activities across four states, and the partnerships behind them.

On the Ground

From Digital Skills Training to Internship Placements: DDM Graduates Take the Next Step

On 14 November, 96 young people graduated from the Design and Digital Marketing School (DDM) in Lagos, trained in Digital Marketing and Graphic Design. Within two weeks, 38% had secured internship placements. DDM is delivered in partnership with the Lagos State Employment Trust Fund (LSETF) and German Development Cooperation (GIZ).

 

SafeOnline: Reaching More Young People with Digital Safety Education

Supported by Meta, SafeOnline has reached more than 20,000 students, helping them recognise online risks, behave responsibly online and build healthier technology habits. Youth Track peer educators lead school and community sessions, making digital safety more relatable for young people.

Young Africa Innovates: Supporting 800+ Innovators Across Four States

Cohort 2 of Young Africa Innovates (YAI) continued bootcamps in Abia, Akwa Ibom, Cross River and Plateau States, where more than 800 young innovators are developing their ideas through structured learning and mentorship. November also saw facilitator and evaluator training on curriculum alignment, inclusive facilitation, safeguarding and programme delivery. YAI is delivered with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the Mastercard Foundation.

 

Get Set for Business: Supporting Enterprise Development in Ogun State

Get Set for Business is now in Week 4 in Ogun State, with 80 participants building business knowledge and practical enterprise skills. It is delivered in partnership with the British Council and King’s Trust International.

Experience AI: Expanding Access to Digital and AI Education

With the Raspberry Pi Foundation and Google DeepMind, we are training 4,000 teachers, including 500 Special Needs teachers, across Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Ondo and Ekiti States to use digital tools and teach AI concepts with confidence, with assistive resources for teachers of learners with additional needs.

Get Set for Business: Oyo State Rollout

Following the ongoing implementation in Ogun State, Get Set for Business is scheduled to commence in Oyo State in December.

The next phase will extend the programme’s business development support to participants in the state.

 

Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria

NerdzFactory Company is also advancing work under Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria, an initiative focused on empowering women through technology skills development and pathways into dignified work.
