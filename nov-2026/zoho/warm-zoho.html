November at NerdzFactory

As the year draws to a close, we are taking stock of the progress made through our programmes and partnerships. November brought important milestones across skills development, youth innovation, digital safety and enterprise development.

From the transition of DDM graduates into internship placements to ongoing engagements with young innovators and students across different states, our work continues to focus on practical outcomes and opportunities.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Here are some of the highlights from November.

On the Ground

From Digital Skills Training to Internship Placements: DDM Graduates Take the Next Step

On 14 November, 96 young people graduated from the Design and Digital Marketing School (DDM) in Lagos, completing training in Digital Marketing and Graphic Design.

The impact is already extending beyond the classroom. Within two weeks of graduation, 38% of the graduates had secured internship placements, marking an important step towards applying their skills in real work environments.

The programme was delivered in partnership with the Lagos State Employment Trust Fund (LSETF) and German Development Cooperation (GIZ).

SafeOnline: Reaching More Young People with Digital Safety Education

Through the Meta-supported SafeOnline Programme, more than 20,000 students have been impacted so far through digital safety and awareness engagements.

The programme continues to equip young people with the knowledge to recognise online risks, practise responsible digital behaviour and develop healthier technology habits.

Through its Youth Track, peer educators support school and community engagements, making digital safety conversations more relatable and accessible to young people.

Young Africa Innovates: Supporting 800+ Innovators Across Four States

The Young Africa Innovates (YAI) Programme, Cohort 2, continued its bootcamp activities across Abia, Akwa Ibom, Cross River and Plateau States.

With more than 800 innovators participating across the four states, the programme is supporting young people to develop and strengthen their innovative ideas through structured learning, practical engagement and mentorship.

In November, facilitator and evaluator training sessions were conducted across the participating states. The sessions covered curriculum alignment, inclusive facilitation, safeguarding and programme delivery.

The programme is implemented by NerdzFactory Company in partnership with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the Mastercard Foundation.

 

Get Set for Business: Supporting Enterprise Development in Ogun State

Get Set for Business continued in Ogun State in November, with 80 participants progressing through the programme.

Now in Week 4, participants are engaging in structured learning to strengthen their business knowledge and practical enterprise skills.

The programme is being implemented by NerdzFactory Company in partnership with the British Council and King’s Trust International.

Recognition & Milestones

NerdzFactory Named Jobs & Talent Catalyst of the Year 2026

NerdzFactory Company was named Jobs & Talent Catalyst of the Year 2026 at the AfriLabs Hub Annual Awards 2026.

The recognition highlights our work in building skills and creating pathways that connect talent to opportunities for growth and employment.

Experience AI: Expanding Access to Digital and AI Education

In partnership with the Raspberry Pi Foundation and Google DeepMind, NerdzFactory Company is working to expand access to foundational digital and AI education for teachers across Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Ondo and Ekiti States.

The initiative targets 4,000 teachers, including 500 Special Needs teachers. Through practical training and tailored learning resources, the programme aims to strengthen teachers’ confidence in using digital tools and introducing AI concepts in the classroom.

The accessibility component also provides assistive learning resources to support teachers working with learners with additional needs.

Get Set for Business: Oyo State Rollout

Following the ongoing implementation in Ogun State, Get Set for Business is scheduled to commence in Oyo State in December.

The next phase will extend the programme’s business development support to participants in the state.

 

Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria

MTN Nigeria Strengthens Child Online Safety Efforts with NerdzFactory Partnership

MTN Nigeria, through its Help Children Be Children initiative, has partnered with NerdzFactory Company to strengthen child online safety awareness and promote safer digital experiences for children and young people across Nigeria.

The collaboration will support conversations that empower young people and equip parents, guardians and educators with the knowledge and tools to navigate an increasingly digital world safely and confidently.

The first activity under the partnership is Your Screen, Your Story, a virtual webinar scheduled for Saturday, 12 December 2026.
