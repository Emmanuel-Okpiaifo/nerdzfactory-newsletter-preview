On the Ground

Design & Digital Marketing School (DDM): Cohort 3 Begins

The Design & Digital Marketing School Lagos Programme, delivered in partnership with the Lagos State Employment Trust Fund (LSETF) and the German Development Cooperation (GIZ), is training 100 Lagos State youths aged 18 to 35 in digital marketing and graphic design.

Now in its third cohort, the programme equips young people with practical skills to pursue employment and other career opportunities in the digital economy. Building on the progress of previous cohorts, the current training continues NerdzFactory’s work to expand access to digital skills and support young people on their career journeys.

SafeOnline: Young People Teaching Young People

SafeOnline 2026 is putting young people at the centre of digital safety education. Through its Youth Track, selected young people are trained and supported by NerdzFactory to become peer educators and community trainers, leading sessions for teenagers aged 13 to 17 in schools and community spaces.

The programme goes beyond online safety awareness. Alongside learning how to recognise digital risks and develop healthier screen-time habits, teenagers are introduced to artificial intelligence (AI), how it works, and how to engage with it responsibly. Through peer-led AI Literacy Circles, they can explore these topics, share experiences and encourage one another to make informed choices online.

By equipping young people to teach their peers, SafeOnline creates a relatable way to build digital safety and AI literacy while encouraging healthier digital habits that extend beyond individual training sessions.

What’s Ahead

Strengthening Teachers’ Digital Literacy

Through our partnership with the Raspberry Pi Foundation and Google DeepMind on the Experience AI programme, NerdzFactory is expanding its work to help teachers build the digital skills they need in today’s classrooms.

The training will reach 4,000 teachers across Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Ondo and Ekiti States, covering essential digital skills and introducing them to artificial intelligence (AI) and how it can be used in learning. This will also include 500 Special Needs teachers, who will receive training tailored to their classroom needs, alongside assistive learning resources to help students with additional learning needs participate more fully in digital education.

Preparing Young Women for Digital Careers

NerdzFactory is preparing to deliver digital skills and employability training for young women aged 18 to 35 under the Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria programme.

The training will focus on Digital Marketing and Data Analytics, combining practical exercises and projects with workplace and employability skills. Delivery is planned across Lagos and Ogun States, helping participants build relevant competencies and prepare for opportunities in Nigeria’s growing business process outsourcing (BPO) and digital services sectors. Through this work, NerdzFactory aims to help young women move beyond learning new skills to becoming better prepared for employment.

Your Screen, Your Story: Promoting Safer Digital Experiences

Through MTN Nigeria’s Help Children Be Children (HCBC) initiative, NerdzFactory is preparing for Your Screen, Your Story, a virtual event focused on helping children and young people navigate the digital world safely and responsibly.

The 2-hour interactive event will bring together young people, parents, guardians and educators to explore topics including cyberbullying, artificial intelligence, online privacy and digital wellbeing. The programme will feature expert discussions, interactive sessions and practical guidance for building safer online habits.

The event is scheduled for Saturday 12th December 2026 at 10:00am WAT.
