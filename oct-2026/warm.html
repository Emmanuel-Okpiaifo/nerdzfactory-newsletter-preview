October at NerdzFactory Company

From digital skills training to safer digital spaces, our work continues to create opportunities for people and communities to learn, grow and thrive. Here are this month’s highlights from the Design & Digital Marketing School and SafeOnline, plus what’s coming next.

On the Ground

DDM School

Cohort 3 Begins

With LSETF and GIZ, the Design & Digital Marketing School Lagos Programme is training 100 Lagos youths aged 18 to 35 in digital marketing and graphic design, building practical skills for jobs in the digital economy.

SafeOnline

Young People Teaching Young People

Through the SafeOnline Youth Track, young peer educators lead sessions for teenagers aged 13 to 17 in schools and communities on spotting online risks, healthier screen time and using AI responsibly, through peer-led AI Literacy Circles.

What’s Ahead

Experience AI

Strengthening Teachers’ Digital Literacy

With the Raspberry Pi Foundation and Google DeepMind, we will train 4,000 teachers across Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Ondo and Ekiti in digital skills and AI for learning, including 500 Special Needs teachers with tailored training and assistive resources.

TTS Nigeria

Preparing Young Women for Digital Careers

Under the Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria programme, we are preparing to train young women aged 18 to 35 in Lagos and Ogun in Digital Marketing and Data Analytics.

Hands-on projects and employability skills will prepare them for roles in Nigeria’s growing BPO and digital services sectors.

MTN Help Children Be Children

Your Screen, Your Story

A 2-hour interactive virtual event for young people, parents, guardians and educators on cyberbullying, AI, online privacy and digital wellbeing, with expert discussions and practical tips for safer online habits.
