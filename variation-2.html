 On the Ground

Indigenous Apprenticeship to Work Programme

The Indigenous Apprenticeship to Work Programme connects young people with practical pathways into work and enterprise.

Through skills development, workplace exposure and practical experience, the programme supports young people as they build confidence and prepare for greater economic participation.

 
 Safer Digital Spaces

SafeOnline

Creating safer digital environments for young people requires more than educating young people themselves. It also requires the adults, institutions and systems around them to understand the digital realities they are navigating.

This month, SafeOnline brought two important groups into the conversation: Parents and Guardians Engagement Session and Policy Actors Dialogue Session.
At Home

Parents & Guardians Engagement Session

Parents and guardians play an important role in helping young people navigate an increasingly digital world.

The engagement explored the opportunities and risks young people encounter online, including emerging technologies and changing digital behaviours. It also focused on practical ways adults can better understand young people’s online experiences, encourage healthier digital habits and have more informed conversations about technology at home.

Across the Ecosystem

Policy Actors Dialogue Session

Young people’s digital experiences are also shaped by the policies, institutions and systems around them.

The Policy Actors engagement brought stakeholders into conversations around digital safety, emerging technologies, AI literacy and the systems needed to better support young people.

The engagement created space to consider existing gaps, share perspectives and explore how different actors can contribute to stronger responses to challenges in digital spaces.
